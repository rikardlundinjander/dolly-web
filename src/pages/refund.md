# Refund Policy

_Last updated: 2026-07-29._

We want you to be happy with **Dolly**. If it doesn't work out, we offer a
**14-day money-back guarantee**: email **support@heydolly.app** within 14 days of
your purchase and we'll refund you in full — no need to explain why.

## How refunds are processed
Dolly is sold through **Polar**, our **Merchant of Record**. Refunds are
issued by Polar back to your original payment method. You can request a
refund by emailing us at **support@heydolly.app**, or directly through the receipt
Polar sent you. Refunds usually appear within 7–14 business days,
depending on your bank or card issuer.

## EU right of withdrawal
If you are a consumer in the EU, you normally have a 14-day right to withdraw
from a purchase of digital content. Because Dolly is delivered digitally and
available immediately, you may be asked at checkout to agree to immediate access
— which can waive that statutory right once the download begins. Our voluntary
14-day guarantee above is offered regardless, and does not limit any mandatory
rights you have under EU or local law.

## After a refund
Once refunded, your license is deactivated and you must stop using and delete
the Software.

## Questions
Email **support@heydolly.app** and we'll help.
