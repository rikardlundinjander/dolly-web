# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2026-07-29._

This Privacy Policy explains how **Rikard Lundin-Jandér** ("we", "us"), the maker of
**Dolly**, handles personal data in the Dolly macOS application and on
**https://heydolly.app**.

## The short version

Dolly is a screen recorder and video editor that runs entirely on your Mac.
**Your recordings never leave your device.** We have no servers that receive
your screen, camera, microphone or project files, and we cannot see what you
record. Dolly contains **no analytics, tracking or telemetry**. The only data
we handle is what is strictly needed to sell you a license, activate it, and
deliver software updates.

## Data we process

### Your recordings and projects — stays on your Mac
Screen, camera and microphone captures, and the projects you save (`.dolly`
files), are stored **locally on your computer**. They are never uploaded to us
or to any third party by the app. You are in full control of these files.

### System permissions the app requests
To record, Dolly asks macOS for the permissions below. The resulting data is
used only on your device to produce your recording and is never transmitted:
- **Screen Recording** — to capture your screen or a window.
- **Camera** — for the optional webcam overlay.
- **Microphone** — for the optional audio track.
- **Accessibility** — optional; only if you enable keystroke display, so the
  app can log which shortcuts you pressed during the recording.

### Purchases
Payments are processed by our reseller **Polar**, which acts as the **Merchant
of Record**. When you buy Dolly, Polar collects and processes your payment and
billing details (for example your email address, and the country/tax
information required for invoicing). **We never receive or store your full
payment-card details.** We receive limited order information such as your email
address, license key and country. See Polar's privacy policy:
<https://polar.sh/legal/privacy>.

### License activation
When you activate Dolly, the app sends your **license key** and a **device
label** — the name of your Mac (e.g. "Anna's MacBook Pro"), which may contain
your name — to Polar's license-key service. This is used only to verify your
license and to count and limit the number of devices an activation is used on.
On later launches the app re-validates the license the same way. No hardware
identifiers, recordings or usage data are sent.

### Software updates
Dolly checks for updates by requesting an update feed from
**https://updates.heydolly.app**. Like any web request, this transmits your **IP
address**, the **app version** and your **macOS version**, and is used only to
offer you the correct update. The feed is hosted on GitHub Pages, so GitHub may
log standard request metadata (see GitHub's privacy statement). Updates are
cryptographically signed; you can turn off automatic checks in the app.

### The website
The website **https://heydolly.app** uses no tracking cookies and no analytics.

### Support
If you email us, we process your message and email address to reply and, where
relevant, keep a record of the correspondence.

## Legal bases (GDPR)
- **Performance of a contract** — selling and licensing Dolly.
- **Legitimate interests** — delivering updates, preventing license abuse, and
  answering support requests.
- **Consent** — for any optional analytics (if introduced), which you can
  withdraw at any time.

## Data retention
Recordings and projects remain on your device until you delete them. Order and
license records are kept for as long as needed to support your purchase and to
meet legal and accounting obligations (these are held largely by Polar as
Merchant of Record).

## International transfers
Polar and GitHub may process data outside the EU/EEA (e.g. in the United
States) under appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.

## Your rights
Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete, restrict, or port
your personal data, and to object to certain processing. To exercise any of
these, contact us at **support@heydolly.app**. You also have the right to lodge
a complaint with your data protection authority (in Sweden, the IMY).

## Children
Dolly is not directed at children under 16 and we do not knowingly collect
their data.

## Changes
We may update this policy; the "last updated" date above will change and, for
material changes, we will note it on the website.
